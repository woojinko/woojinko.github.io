About Me

My name is Woojin, and I recently graduated from a graduate program in Computer Science at Cornell Tech. I was a member of the XR Collaboratory.

My research was in human-computer interaction and applied AI , with a focus on the intersection of technology and physical / mental health. I worked on the development and design process of applications, mostly in the domains of Virtual and Augmented Reality (VR / AR), mobile, and web. My past projects have spanned various areas such as AI research, healthcare, architectural design, music, and more.

Previously, I graduated from UC Berkeley with a B.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science as a member of the Berkeley EECS Honors Program. I worked under the supervision of Prof. Bjoern Hartmann in the Jacobs Institute for Design Innovation, Prof. Luisa Caldas in the XR Lab, and Dr. Allen Yang in the FHL Vive Center for Enhanced Reality

I have worked as a freelancer for various gigs, including one as an AI research fellow at OpenAI, interactive technologist at Bravo Media., and technical exhibit designer at the National Museum of Mathematics.
